# THUNDER OF THORN

**A cinematic dark-fantasy anthem about surviving what was meant to break you.**

> *If the storm wants a crown, let it take mine—*  
> *I was born where the roots drink lightning.*

## The concept

A lone voice walks through a dead kingdom as a storm gathers overhead. The thorns are every scar, every betrayal, every hard-won boundary. The thunder is the moment the narrator stops running and turns to face the sky.

The emotional turn is the heart of the song: **the storm doesn't make the hero powerful; it reveals the power that was already there.**

## Chorus

Let the thunder call my name,  
Let the black sky split in two.  
I have worn the thorn, I have walked the flame—  
Now the storm has to go through me.  

Raise the lightning, wake the stone,  
Hear the wild roots answer below.  
If the night wants blood, let it come—  
I am the storm it made its own.

## Short-form edit idea

**Length:** 20–25 seconds  
**Opening text:** “They thought the storm would break me.”

- **0��4s:** Quiet close-up; wind and a distant thunder rumble. Keep the image nearly still.
- **4–8s:** A hand closes around a thorn-covered branch; the first pulse of the beat appears.
- **8–12s:** On “I have worn the thorn,” cut between quick flashes of rain, armor, and a figure turning toward camera.
- **12–20s:** Drop into “Now the storm has to go through me.” Hit the strongest visual on “through me”: lightning flash, hard cut, then a wide shot of the figure standing in the storm.
- **Final frame:** “I am the storm it made its own.” Hold for a beat, then cut to black.

Use a slow build, one deliberate impact on the chorus, and captions timed tightly to the vocal. Keep the first few seconds quiet enough to create contrast; let the chorus earn the spectacle.

## Sound and visual direction

- **Sound:** Start with intimate vocals, low strings, and distant storm texture. Build with heavy toms and layered guitars or dark orchestral percussion. Make the chorus wide and anthemic, then leave a brief breath before the final line.
- **Palette:** Charcoal, storm-blue, silver lightning, and a small accent of thorn-red.
- **Typography:** Bold, condensed lettering; minimal words on screen; high contrast for readability on mobile.
- **Mood:** Defiant, mythic, wounded but never defeated.

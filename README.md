# Thunder of Thorn

A short creative edit inspired by the song title "Thunder of Thorn".

## Overview

Thunder of Thorn is a dramatic, storm-laced concept built around tension, defiance, and the echo of ancient power. The imagery evokes a sky split by lightning, roots twisting through stone, and a heartbeat carried by the wind.

## Stanza

The storm climbs over broken earth,
With iron rain and thunder's breath.
A thorned path lifts toward the light,
Where shadows learn to stand and fight.

The night may howl, the world may bend,
But hope still rises from the end.
And every spark beneath the sky
Keeps burning where the wild winds cry.
